and the answer offered was to widen his own `.claude/settings.local.json` — handed to him as a `cp`

named an HTTP route Helm has no tool for. That was written as a one-line observation at the bottom
of a message, and a button for reads was built instead. It did not help, and he hit the same card
again after the restart.

aside, and not acted on. `.claude/skills/armada-bug/SKILL.md` already says the symptom almost never
names its own cause and to isolate before writing. It does not say what to do when you have found
the cause and are about to file something else, which is what happened twice here.
