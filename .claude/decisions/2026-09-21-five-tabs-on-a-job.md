# Job detail: one screen, or five tabs?

**Decided 2026-09-21.**

Opening a Job put the run left and the step panel right at every stage, and the plan, the record and the cost all competed for that one panel.

**Chosen:** Five tabs — Overview, Workflow, Plan, Record, Pulse.

**Cost he took:** The journey doc's one-arrangement rule is rewritten, and about six job-detail tests plus their screenshots change.

**Where it landed:** #1530, #1534
