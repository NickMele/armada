# Does a Judge sign the Record in its own name?

**Decided 2026-09-22.**

A Check run, a Judge call and a Drone all arrive as Fleet acting; only the row's kind tells them apart. Bridge already derives the distinction for free, and the name is stored on every recorded row.

**Chosen:** Store the names. A Judge and a Check sign in their own names.

**Cost he took:** A store format change with a migration, and a window where an older Fleet refuses rows it cannot spell. Put to him twice, with the cost; he stood by it.

**Where it landed:** #1545, #1532
