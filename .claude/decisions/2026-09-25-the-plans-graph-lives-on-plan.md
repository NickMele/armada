# Where does the plan's graph live?

**Decided 2026-09-25.**

Workflow drew the whole plan: the steps, the groups hanging off the step that
wrote them, the tasks hanging off their groups. He pinned a note on it asking
for one *Plan* node summary instead, and a second note asking the Plan tab for
views of its own — Graph, List, and a diagram of the repository showing where a
plan's groups and tasks fall and what overlaps.

Shown that one reading of the two notes kills the second edge, and offered a
fold-in-place arrangement that would have kept it:

**Chosen:** Workflow draws the workflow's steps and a single *Plan* node that
opens the Plan tab. The plan's graph becomes Plan's Graph view, beside the List
it already has.

**Cost he took:** The second edge dies. On the Plan tab there are no step nodes,
so nothing can draw *and this step worked it* — which was the whole of
`2026-09-23-one-node-two-edges.md`, two days earlier. The group and task node
work is also rebuilt in a new place. Both stated, both taken.

**Where it landed:** annotations `20260925-143646-k4cs` and
`20260925-143835-gese`. Narrows `2026-09-23-one-node-two-edges.md`, which stays
as the record of what was traded away.

**What it says about him:** he reversed a two-day-old decision of his own, in
the direction of less on one screen, and paid for it with a relation he had
explicitly asked to see. The screen being readable beat the picture being
complete — see [[2026-09-22-helm-overlays]], where the same trade went the same
way.
