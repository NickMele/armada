# Does a screen explain itself?

**Decided 2026-09-23.**

Every screen built for the Job redesign carried explanatory prose beside its facts — how the bar fills, why a process is named by its executable, that size on disk is a fact about a checkout, what a group boundary is. The owner's words: *"I hate it, I just want the app to show facts. Any hints or guides should be something I choose to see."*

**Chosen:** the app shows facts. An explanation lives behind a `?` beside the thing it explains, opens as a card, and is catalogued so it can be read again later — the way No Man's Sky and Crimson Desert do it.

**Cost he took:** a reader who does not know the vocabulary has to press something to learn it, and every explanation now needs a home rather than a sentence.

**Where it landed:** `.claude/skills/how-the-owner-decides/`, and the issue filed from it.
