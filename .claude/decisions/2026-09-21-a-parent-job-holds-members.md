# Three PRs landing in order: one Job or three?

**Decided 2026-09-21.**

A change needing three pull requests in order had no page, and the doc described it three different ways.

**Chosen:** A parent Job that holds the plan and takes one approval, complete when every member has landed.

**Cost he took:** Completion has to mean the pull request merged rather than the step approved.

**Where it landed:** #1530, #1543
