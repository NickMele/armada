# How does a person learn what Armada's pieces are?

**Decided 2026-09-23.**

Nothing teaches a person what a group boundary, a refusal or a Job with members is — you learn Armada by reading its source or by asking. The owner: *"Video games do such a great job of guiding players through a game. It's how players learn the game. Armada doesn't need gamification but animations and guidance can help a user understand how the pieces work."*

**Chosen:** teach the way a game teaches. A card opens the first time you meet a piece, once — **and the first card you ever see offers to turn all of them off.** After that the `?` is how you ask and the catalogue is where you read. Motion shows how pieces relate, where the relation is the thing being learned. **No gamification**: no points, no streaks, no badges, no progress for its own sake.

**Cost he took:** something appears that he did not ask for, at the moment he is trying to read a screen. The one appearance is paid for by being dismissible forever from the first card.

**Where it landed:** #1603, beside #1602's `?` and catalogue.
