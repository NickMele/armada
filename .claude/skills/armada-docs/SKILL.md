## Open questions

Armada's open questions live in the Armada Questions database in Notion. Before filing a question, answering one, or editing a page that carries an open-question link, read Open Questions Procedure. Never answer a question by editing a page without following it.

A question carries a **Lands on** table naming every page and section that changes when it is answered, with a tick per site. Walking that table is the work. A question is Applied when every row is ticked, and not before.

## The rules most often skipped

- **A page states what is true.** It never says a decision was made, when, or by whom. No date stamps in body text, no "resolved", no "amended".
- **Never assert what a decision is from memory, from a summary, or from what the conversation says.** Read the row.
- **Every fact has one owner and nothing else restates it.** A concept page never restates another concept's decisions. A capability says whether something is built; a concept says what it means.
- **A fact three pages need is a database row, not a sentence on three pages.**
- **Propose, wait for an explicit yes, then write to Notion.**
- **One decision at a time**, as numbered options with a recommendation.
